Prepped support

Prepped keeps track of the supplies you have at home and shows how many days they would last your household. It works without an account and without the internet.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone, in the app's own storage, and nowhere else. The app has no account and sends nothing to anyone.

How do I keep a copy, or move to a new iPhone?

Open Settings (the gear on the Readiness tab) and tap "Back up now". Save the file somewhere you trust, such as the Files app. On the new iPhone, open Settings in Prepped, tap "Restore from a backup" and choose that file.

The app asks the iPhone to leave its storage out of iCloud and computer backups, so a backup file you make yourself is the only copy. The file is not locked with a password, so keep it somewhere safe.

How do I delete everything?

In Settings, tap "Start fresh". Deleting the app from your iPhone also deletes everything it kept there. Backup files you saved stay where you put them until you delete them.

What is a readiness score?

It is this app's own way of counting what you have logged. It is not an official standard. Each category's screen shows what its score covers and what is not logged yet.

Does the app give advice?

No. Prepped tracks what you have. It gives no medical, legal or tactical advice.
