Prepped privacy policy

Updated November 17, 2026

In short

This app keeps what you enter on your device. It has no account, no ads and no tracking, and it sends nothing to its maker or to anyone else.

What the app keeps

The supplies you log, with their amounts and dates. The people and pets in your household, with any details you add, such as calories a day, personal needs and food allergies. Your answers about your home, and your settings.

All of it is kept in the app's own storage on your device. The app does not ask for document numbers, account details, cash amounts or firearm serial numbers.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you send it. The app does not use the internet.

One thing makes a file you can save or share: a backup, which holds everything you entered. You choose where the file goes. A backup file is not locked with a password, so anyone who has the file can read it.

On iPhone, the app asks the phone to leave its storage out of iCloud and computer backups of the device.

Reminders

Reminders you turn on are set up on your device and shown by it. None is sent from a server.

Keeping and deleting

What you enter stays until you delete it. You can delete any entry, person or pet in the app. On iPhone, deleting the app deletes everything it kept there.

Files you saved or shared stay where you put them until you delete them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new text appears here with its date.

Questions
